WA Audio Listener

A small personal tool that files audio from a dedicated WhatsApp number into the owner's Google Drive, dated by the Hebrew calendar.

What it does

WA Audio Listener connects one dedicated WhatsApp Business number to one Google Drive folder. It is a personal, single-user tool: only the owner uses it, on the owner's own accounts.

  • Someone sends a voice note or audio file to the dedicated WhatsApp number.
  • The app picks up that audio.
  • It saves the audio into a folder called WhatsApp Audio in the owner's Google Drive, sorted into subfolders by the caption or category on the message.
  • Each file is named with the Hebrew calendar date it was received.

That is the whole app. It does not read, send, or analyze anything else.

Who runs it

WA Audio Listener is operated by DaysWon for its owner's personal use. It is not a public service and has no other users.
