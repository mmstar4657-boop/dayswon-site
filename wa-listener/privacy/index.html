Privacy Policy

WA Audio Listener · DaysWon

Last updated: November 12, 2026

The short version

WA Audio Listener is a personal tool used by one person (the owner). It saves audio files sent to a dedicated WhatsApp number into the owner's own Google Drive. It collects nothing else, shares nothing, sells nothing, and runs no analytics.

What Google data the app accesses

The app connects to Google Drive only, using Google OAuth sign-in. With that access it can create and read files in its own folder, called WhatsApp Audio, inside the owner's Google Drive account.

The app does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Photos, or any other Google service. It does not read other files in the Drive account beyond its own folder.

How the data is used

The Google Drive access is used for exactly one thing: storing the audio files the app receives (voice notes and audio files sent to the dedicated WhatsApp number), organized into subfolders and named by the Hebrew calendar date. Nothing else.

Where data is stored

  • Audio files are stored in the owner's Google Drive, in the WhatsApp Audio folder. They stay under the owner's Google account.
  • OAuth tokens (the credentials that let the app reach Google Drive) are stored only on the app's own server. They are never shared with anyone.

What the app does not do

  • No selling of data, ever.
  • No sharing of data with third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No other users: it is a single-user, personal tool.

How to revoke access

You can disconnect the app from Google at any time in your Google account settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from reaching Google Drive immediately.

How to request deletion

To have stored audio files or any other app data deleted, email dealswithease@gmail.com and it will be taken care of.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
